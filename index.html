WHAT WE DO

We turn shop-floor machines into living 3D

A digital twin is an exact 3D copy of a real machine you can open on any screen — every part named, every view a click away, and, with sensors connected, showing what the real machine is doing right now.

01

3D modelling

Accurate models built from site photos, measurements and drawings.

02

Interactive 3D viewer

Rotate, zoom and click any part — in a web browser, on any device.

03

Exploded & line views

Pull parts apart, and print clean top, front and side drawings.

04

Live digital twin

Sensors stream real readings into the model through Arduino / IoT.

05

Simulation

Watch the machine run: belts move, sensors trigger, counts update.

06

Training & manuals

Part guides and walkthroughs, made from the same model.

CASE STUDY

Smart conveyor → digital twin

Our first build: a working sensor conveyor, modelled part by part and brought to life with its own live readings.

  • 16 modelled parts — from Arduino to rollers
  • 8 preset views plus a 4-view drawing layout
  • Exploded, X-ray and line-trace modes
  • Belt, IR and ultrasonic simulation
  • Live distance, temperature, humidity and count
